How It Works

Step 1: The viewer opens the application and clicks "START STREAM"

Step 2: A unique link is generated and sent to the target device

Step 3: The target opens the link and grants camera access

Step 4: The viewer receives the live camera feed and can record, take snapshots, and monitor statistics

System Requirements

Browser Support: Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+, Opera 76+

Bandwidth: 2-4 Mbps recommended for Medium quality (720p)

Devices: Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices with camera access

Security & Privacy

Remote Camera uses peer-to-peer technology, meaning your video stream goes directly between devices without passing through our servers. Optional encryption ensures your data stays private. No recordings are stored on any server - everything stays on your device.
